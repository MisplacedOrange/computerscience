Average ML Engineer

Responsibilities

  • Collaborate with product teams, engineers, and data scientists to design and deploy ML and GenAI solutions.
  • Communicate ML concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders and support alignment on use cases and solutions.
  • Identify opportunities to apply ML and GenAI to improve business outcomes.
  • Build, integrate, and maintain scalable ML services, data pipelines, and LLM workflows using Python and related tools.
  • Use AWS (primary) and Azure (secondary) for deployment, data infrastructure, and ML services.

Qualifications

  • 3+ years of experience in machine learning or software engineering
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Data Science, or a related field
  • Strong Python programming and software engineering skills
  • Experience applying machine learning and Generative AI to real-world problems
  • Knowledge of testing, version control, and performance optimization
  • Familiar with Docker, Kubernetes, and cloud platforms like AWS or Azure

Benefits

  • Depending on the position, you may receive bonuses from commission earnings, discretionary bonuses, long-term incentives, and other benefits.

Senior ML Engineer

Responsibilities

  • Build and deploy ML and Generative AI systems.
  • Work with large-scale datasets such as banking and conversation data.
  • Improve automation, reliability, and operational efficiency.
  • Continuously learn and collaborate with AI experts.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in a related field
  • 3+ years of software development experience
  • Strong ML/deep learning knowledge
  • Skilled in Python, Java, C, or C++
  • Strong software design and API development skills
  • Experience with frameworks like PyTorch, TensorFlow, Jax, or LangGraph preferred

Benefits

  • Competitive salary and bonus opportunities
  • Health, wellness, retirement, and savings programs
  • Paid time off and banking discounts
  • Career development and recognition programs
